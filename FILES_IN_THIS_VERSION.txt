FIXES A TIMER LEFT RUNNING PAST MIDNIGHT

The bug: a timer running overnight put ALL of its time — last night's hours
too — on the next day. The log entry was dated the night before, so Notes
showed nothing for today, and deleting it said "today's totals are
unaffected" and took nothing off.

Why: the midnight cut relied on the background service remembering the date
in memory. Samsung phones restart background services overnight, and a
restarted service thought it was already today, so midnight went unnoticed.

Now:
  - A running timer is cut at every midnight it crossed, worked out from when
    the run started — a restart can't hide it. Last night's part goes to
    yesterday; the part after midnight is today's, with its own entry in
    Notes.
  - This is checked when the app opens, while the timer runs, and before
    starting or stopping a timer.
  - Deleting an entry in Notes goes by when the run ENDED, so an older
    overnight entry stopped today comes off today's total.

TODAY'S 8 HOURS: this build stops it happening again but doesn't rewrite
today's figure. Either:
  - long-press the right-hand column of that row and tap −1h in the blue
    Time row until it's right, or
  - after installing v99, delete last night's entry in that label's Notes
    (it's dated yesterday) — it will now come off today.
