  1. Long-press the TIMER column and slide to correct recorded time.
     Recorded through the same path as the popup's +/- buttons: on the running
     label it folds into that run's row; on an idle one it writes its own
     adjustment record. Nothing saves until you lift your finger, and a label
     can't go below zero for the day.

  2. Both sliders now step a flat 5 minutes.
     The accelerating ladder meant you had to remember how far you'd come to
     know what the next step would add.

  3. The timer auto-sizes between 20 and 32sp.
     "1:09:07" was overflowing a fixed 32sp and showing as "1:09:0". Only rows
     that need it shrink; short timers stay large.
