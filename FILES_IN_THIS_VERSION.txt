WEEK SCREEN

  Three days at a time, larger text (14sp). About nine blocks show; each day
  scrolls for the rest.

  ‹ ›  move one day per tap, carrying on into the next or previous week.
  Tap the dates to jump with a date picker. "Week…" actions (pull in the
  default week, fill from a layout, delete) act on the week of the leftmost
  day shown.

  GESTURES
    tap                     "Move above…" list, like the main screen
    hold, then drag ↕       move within the day
    hold, then drag ↔       change duration, 5 minutes a step
    hold, and let go        menu: exact duration, move to another day, delete
    quick swipe             scroll the day

  THE HOLD is now a real, short timer — a quarter of a second. When it's
  ready the block lights up amber and gives a small buzz. Before, there was no
  deliberate hold; the drag and the scroll were racing for your finger, which
  is why it felt long and unpredictable.

  WHILE DRAGGING
    sideways   " +15    45m Fitness"   — change on the left, new total right
    up/down    "↑2 9:15 45m Fitness"   — places moved, and its new start time

  Parked blocks are in the "Move above…" list, so picking the first parked one
  puts a block last among the live ones.

APPLYING A PLAN

  A parked block now sets its label's goal to ZERO when the plan is applied —
  parked means "not today". A label that isn't in the plan at all keeps its
  goal. (You didn't answer this one, so it went in with the default I
  suggested. Say if you'd rather it didn't.)
