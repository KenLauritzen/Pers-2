Fine vertical lines now mark the boundaries.

THE GOAL SLIDER
  Long-press the right column and slide. Up adds, down subtracts. The ladder
  is 5, 15, 30, 45 minutes, then 15 more for each further step. A step is
  about 24dp of travel, so 45 minutes is roughly 96dp of drag.

  Nothing is saved until you lift your finger. The column shows the pending
  goal in amber while you slide, so the preview sits under the finger setting
  it. The list is held still for the duration.

  On release, if you're sorting by Goals or Remain, the row moves to its new
  place.

ALSO CHANGED
  Tapping a running timer now stops it. Before, it did nothing.
  Long-press on the row as a whole no longer opens the popup — that moved to a
  tap on the right column.

ALSO INCLUDED — the fix held back from v44:
  A goal passed by less than a minute showed a lone "-". It now shows 0:00 in
  peach until a full minute over, then -0:01. The Σ sums had the same fault
  under a minute and are fixed too.
