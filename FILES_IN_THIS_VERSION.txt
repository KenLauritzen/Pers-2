A PROGRESS BAR UNDER EACH TASK'S FIGURES

A slim bar sits under the top line of every task — on the main screen rows
and in the task editor — showing time spent against the estimate.

    ▸ 30m / 45m  66%
    ████████████████░░░░░░░░   blue: two-thirds of the estimate used

    ✓ 56m / 30m  186%
    ████████████████████████   yellow: past the estimate

    · min?
    ████████████████████████   red: no estimate yet

The bar sits UNDER the text rather than behind it, so the figures stay exactly
as readable as before — tinting behind the text was measured first and made it
hard to read in daylight. It's drawn on a dark track so it shows clearly on
both green and dark rows.
