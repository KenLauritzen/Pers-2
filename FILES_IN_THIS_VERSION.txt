While sliding, the change so far (+10, -15) now shows to the left of the figure
being changed, clear of your finger. It disappears on release.

  sliding the GOAL  -> delta appears in the timer column
  sliding the TIME  -> delta appears in place of the label

The label's text is replaced for the duration rather than appended to — you
know which row you're touching, and appending would just truncate.

Note on why not the second line under the label: with no secondary figure
chosen that line is hidden and rows are 44dp; making it appear mid-drag needs
about 45dp, so the row would clip for as long as your finger was down. The
label line costs no height.
