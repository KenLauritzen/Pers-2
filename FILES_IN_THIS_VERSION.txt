Focus v57 — complete file list.

  1. The reminder sound has never worked. Fixed.

     A notification channel's sound and importance are fixed when it's created
     — calling createNotificationChannel again on the same id does nothing. The
     code picked between two channel ids for heads-up vs quiet, but applied the
     SOUND toggle to the same id either way. So whatever the setting was the
     first time you ever ran the app is what you've had since.

     There are now three channels, one per combination, chosen when the
     notification is sent rather than edited: pop-up with sound, pop-up silent,
     quiet. New ids, and the old ones are deleted so they don't linger in
     Android's notification settings.

     If sound still doesn't play, check Android Settings > Apps > Focus >
     Notifications — "Timer reminders" should be there with a sound set.

  2. The ongoing notification is ordered, and shorter.

     Was library order, which is to say none. Now:
       1. the running label
       2. labels with a goal, most time remaining first
       3. everything else, most time recorded first

     Labels with neither a goal nor any recorded time are left out — they were
     most of the list and said nothing.
