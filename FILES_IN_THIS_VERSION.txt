RANGE TOTALS (idea 72)

Settings -> "Totals" opens a new screen: hours by label between two dates.

  Sun 7 Sep  →  now

    Work           18:45   45%  ██████████
    App Dev        12:10   29%  ██████░░░░
    Planning        4:30   11%  ██░░░░░░░░
    Nutrition       0:00        ░░░░░░░░░░

    Total          40:50
    7 days  ·  5:50 a day

  Defaults to the most recent Sunday at midnight. Tap either end for a date
  picker.

  Labels with no time in the range are shown, dimmed, at the bottom — absence
  is information.

  The bar is each label's SHARE, scaled against the largest, not progress
  toward a goal. Goals are daily and mean nothing across a fortnight.

  The average line matters: 40:50 across a week is 5:50 a day, across a
  fortnight less than 3. The total alone is ambiguous.

  h:mm throughout — seconds are noise over a week.

NOT INCLUDED: task totals.
  TaskStore keeps a lifetime figure per task with no timestamps, so there's no
  way to know how much of it fell inside your dates. A number that ignored the
  range on a range screen would mislead. It needs task time logged with
  timestamps first.
