SEED…     fill your column from a 7-day range, or every label at zero.
          From a range: planned and actual side by side, planned chosen.
          Tap a row to take the other figure for that label only.

HAND OVER hides your column, the difference and Together, so Janice fills
          hers without seeing yours. "Show both" brings them back.

MORE…     add a label only Janice has · sleep goals for each of you ·
          plan with someone else

ADJUSTING ANY FIGURE
  tap             exact: −5h −1h −15m −5m / +5m +15m +1h +5h
  hold, drag ↔    15 minutes a step; the change and new total show under
                  the title while you drag

COLOURS
  teal = you, lavender = Janice, amber = Together
  Diff is coloured by whoever wants more — a difference isn't a problem.
  Together goes RED, along with the figure it outruns, when it's more than
  either of you planned for that label.
