Focus v59 — complete file list.

The -5m / +5m buttons are gone. Long-pressing a row and sliding replaced them,
and they only ever worked on the running timer anyway.

In their place, a third total. The bottom row now has one under each column:

  under the label column   total for the SECOND figure (the sub-line)
  under the timer column   total time recorded
  under the right column   total for the PRIMARY figure

Each matches the column above it, prefixes and colours included — so you get
Σ8:35 in mint, or ~2:15p in grey-blue.

The clock modes can't sum — adding two times of day means nothing — so Start
and ETA show WHEN YOU WOULD FINISH instead: the day's start plus every goal, or
now plus everything still to do.

The left total is blank when the second figure is set to None.
